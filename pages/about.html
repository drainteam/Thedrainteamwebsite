Our Story

DUBLIN-BORN,
DUBLIN-BASED.

The Drain Team was founded in 2009 by two qualified plumbing engineers who had grown tired of seeing Dublin homeowners overcharged for poor-quality drainage work. The idea was simple: build a company that shows up on time, charges a fair price, and doesn't leave until the job is done properly.

Fifteen years later, that same principle drives everything we do. We've grown from a two-van operation to a team of 12 qualified engineers covering Dublin city, county and surrounding areas — but the values haven't changed. You'll still get an honest quote upfront, a fully trained engineer at your door, and a job you can rely on.

We work with homeowners, landlords, letting agents, restaurants, offices and local authorities. From a blocked kitchen sink to a full drainage survey for a new commercial development — if it involves water moving through pipes in Dublin, we've handled it.
