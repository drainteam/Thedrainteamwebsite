Our Story

The Drain Team was built from years of hands-on experience in the drainage industry.

Alan Byrne and Sean Woods started their careers on the tools, working their way up through the industry and gaining experience in high-pressure jetting, CCTV surveys, patch repairs, smoke testing, excavation works and drainage installations.

Over the years, they progressed into supervisory roles, managing teams of over 30 people, training new operatives, planning works and ensuring projects were completed to a high standard.

Today, they bring that same practical knowledge and problem-solving approach to every job. At The Drain Team, we don’t just identify problems. We find the solution and carry out the work needed to fix them.
